Privacy policy
LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 11, 2026

This policy explains how LogicSprint: Brain Games handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your high scores, settings, leaderboard display name and a cached copy of the leaderboard are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Global leaderboard (optional)

No account or login is required. If you submit a score from the Result screen, the app sends your display name, score, game, difficulty, app version and submission time to our leaderboard database, hosted by Supabase. Submitted scores are public.

Advertising

LogicSprint shows ads through Google AdMob, which may process your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for consent first.

What we do not collect

No email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or analytics SDKs.
