01 · Movement and scoring

Fly the lower half

The top half stays clear: that's where rocks appear, and you need to see them coming. Within the lower half you can go anywhere, and the higher you fly, the less time you have to react, so height pays.

How it moves

  • Touch anywhere; the ship flies to a point about one thumb above your finger, so your finger never hides it.
  • Allowed band: from the halfway line (50% of the height) to 90%. A faint line marks the top of the band.
  • Same top speed as today (1.8 screen widths a second), now on both axes, easing in as it arrives. It slightly leans in the direction it's moving.

How it's scored

  • Every rock that falls past: 10 × altitude. Altitude is ×1.0 at the bottom of the band, rising evenly to ×1.5 at the halfway line. The HUD shows it live.
  • Close call: a rock that passes within half a ship-width without hitting gives +5, with a small flash.
  • Theme changes stay every 1,250 points. One hit ends the run; revives work as today.

Fair hit rules

  • Today a hit is "crossed the rocket's row near it". In 2D that's not enough, so a hit becomes circle against ship shape, checked along the rock's path each frame (so fast rocks can't skip through).
  • The ship's hit shape is a bit smaller than its drawing (about 80%), so a hit always looks like a hit.
  • The revive shield and the calm 3 s after a revive stay as they are.

Why height pays (reaction time)

Rocks fall at 0.32 screen heights a second at the start and about 0.54 once the storm builds. The time between a rock appearing and reaching the ship:

Ship heightAltitudeReaction time, calmReaction time, storm
Bottom of band (90%)×1.03.0 s1.8 s
Middle (70%)×1.252.3 s1.4 s
Halfway line (50%)×1.51.7 s1.0 s

At the top of the band you have about half the reaction time for 50% more points. Today's fixed row (85%) sits near the bottom, so a cautious player loses nothing compared with now.

02 · The background

Five layers of space

Depth comes from layers moving at different speeds (parallax). Each layer also shifts a few pixels against the ship's position, so the whole scene leans as you fly. Nearer layers lean more. Everything stays behind the rocks, and nothing moves near the bottom half's edge in a way that could be mistaken for a rock.

NebulaSoft colour clouds in the theme's colours, drifting very slowly. Fades from blue to violet to gold as themes change.0.02× · lean 4 px
PlanetsToday's planets (gas giant, ringed, moon orbit, cloud world), unchanged, on their own layer.0.05× · lean 8 px
Far starsMany tiny, dim dots, slow.0.15× · lean 6 px
Near starsFewer, brighter, faster. Stretch into speed lines as the storm builds.0.8× · lean 18 px
Ship glow and trailEngine flame that grows when you climb, and a short fading trail behind the ship.with the ship

Comfort and speed

  • Reduce motion: no leaning and no speed lines; stars still drift slowly so it isn't frozen.
  • Shooting stars stay (they're today's best detail) and still burst on planets.
  • All layers are painted in one CustomPainter per layer with cached star positions, so it stays at 60 fps on older phones.

Optional: tilt

  • Later, the lean could also follow the phone's tilt (gyroscope), so moving the phone moves the stars a little. Off by default, a switch in Settings, and never affects play.
