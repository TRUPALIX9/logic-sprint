Line Split

A line, and a number: ½. Touch the line where half is. Then thirds and quarters, then ⅜, then 0.43 and 37%, on lines that turn, shrink and lie to you with fake tick marks. Three strikes and the run is over.

01 · One mode that ramps up

From ½ to 0.43

No Easy/Medium/Hard: like Rocket Launch and Guess Color, the run itself gets harder, so there's one leaderboard. Each stage adds one new kind of target and keeps the old ones. Lines also start turning ("different ways"): flat, upright, then any slope, always labelled 0 and 1 so the direction is clear.

Halves

Q1–6 · flat lines · ±5%

½. A long flat line. It teaches the gesture; almost everyone gets these.

Thirds and quarters

Q7–14 · any direction · ±5%

⅓ ⅔ ¼ ¾. Lines turn upright and diagonal and get shorter. Thirds are where people start to drift.

Eighths and fifths

Q15–24 · ±5.5%

⅜ ⅝ ⅛ ⅞ ⅖ ⅗. You have to halve a half, or picture fifths with nothing to anchor them.

Decimals and percents

Q25–36 · ±6% · timer 5 s

0.43 · 0.68 · 37%. Numbers that aren't nice fractions. The timer starts here, and the targets avoid easy fractions.

Tricks

Q37–50 · ±6% · timer 4 s

0.3 from the right (count from the other end), and lines with fake tick marks in the wrong places to pull your eye.

Everything, faster

Q51+ · ±5.5% · timer 3.5 s

Any of the above, shorter lines, less time.

02 · Scoring and fairness

How it's scored

Error is measured as a share of the line's length, so a short line and a long line are judged the same. The window never drops below the size of a fingertip.

Per question

error = |where you let go − target| ÷ line length inside the window → HIT +10 error ≤ 2% → SHARP +5 extra error ≤ 1% → BULLSEYE +10 extra outside the window → a strike (3rd strike ends the run) time runs out → a strike every 5 hits in a row → +20 (the app's streak bonus)

Why strikes: estimation isn't right-or-wrong like Guess Color. Being 7% off is "a bit off", so, like Angle Sense, you get three strikes. Revives work on top, as in every game.

Why these windows

Typical adult error (one standard deviation, as % of the line): halving about 1.8%, thirds and quarters about 3%, eighths and fifths about 4%, arbitrary decimals about 4.5%, counting from the other end about 5%. Add fingertip error, about 4 points, which is 1.3% on a 300-point line and 2.5% on a short one.

03 · Screen design

Target, line, reveal

The target sits big above the card (a real stacked fraction, not "3/8" text). The line is the only thing on the card. On reveal, a pink mark shows the true point, a dashed teal mark shows yours, and the gap between them is drawn so the error is obvious.

06 · Decide before building

Open questions

OrderLine Split is game 6 here, True or False game 7. Line Split is smaller to build (no sentence generator), so it can ship first.
Lift or LockAnswer on lift (fast, one gesture, used in the prototype) or place-then-Lock (slower, fewer accidental answers). Lift with a 0.2 s "hold still" is a middle way.
Curved linesLater stages could split an arc or a zig-zag by length. Fun and hard, but much harder to judge fairly; keep for an update.
