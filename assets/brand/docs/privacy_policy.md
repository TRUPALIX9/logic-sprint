# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 11, 2026_

This policy explains how LogicSprint: Brain Games ("LogicSprint", "the app") handles information. LogicSprint is developed by Trupal Patel.

## Information stored on your device

Your high scores, settings (sound, vibration, theme), leaderboard display name, and a cached copy of the leaderboard are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app deletes them. You can clear your high scores at any time in **Settings → Reset High Scores**.

## Global leaderboard (optional)

No account or login is required. If you choose to submit a score from the Result screen, the app sends your chosen display name, score, game, difficulty, app version, and submission time to our leaderboard database, hosted by Supabase. Submitted scores are public: anyone using the app can see the top scores and their display names. Do not use your real name or other personal information as your display name.

## Advertising

LogicSprint shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy Choices**. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

## What we do not collect

LogicSprint does not ask for or collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location, and it does not use analytics SDKs.

## Children

LogicSprint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting leaderboard entries

To have a leaderboard entry removed, contact us with the display name, game, and approximate submission date.

## Changes

We will update this page when this policy changes and revise the date above.
