# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

This policy explains how LogicSprint: Brain Games ("LogicSprint", "the app") handles information. LogicSprint is developed by Trupal Patel.

## Information stored on your device

Your high scores and their run times, your run history, your settings (sound, vibration), your display name, and a cached copy of the leaderboard are stored on your device. Your run history never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app deletes all of this. You can clear your high scores at any time in **Settings → Reset High Scores**.

## Your player profile and the global leaderboard

No email, password or login is required. The first time the app talks to the leaderboard, it creates an anonymous account for your device with our database provider, Supabase. When you finish a game, the app sends the game, difficulty, score and how long the run took, so we can keep your best score and the number of times you have played each game.

If you choose a display name, it is shown publicly next to your best scores on the global Top 10, and it may appear in public game statistics (for example, "best score and games played") on the developer's website. Do not use your real name or other personal information as your display name. Names containing offensive words are refused. You can report another player's name by long-pressing it on the leaderboard; a name reported by several players is hidden from the leaderboard.

LogicSprint is available on Android (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store). This policy covers both.

## Advertising

LogicSprint shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads (on the menus, the Result screen, and in the top bar of the game screens), and optional video ads you can choose to watch for an extra life, to refresh the leaderboard, or to earn hearts. To serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. When you have no internet connection the app shows no ads at all.

Hearts and revives are tracked only on your device and are never sold or linked to your identity.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy Choices**.

- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
- **iPhone:** the app asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used for personalized ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google shows ads that are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

## What we do not collect

LogicSprint does not ask for or collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location, and it does not use analytics SDKs.

## Children

LogicSprint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.

To delete your player profile, display name and scores from the leaderboard, open **Settings → Delete leaderboard data** in the app. This permanently deletes your anonymous account and everything linked to it on our servers; your next game starts a new, empty profile. You can also email us with your display name and its 4-digit code (for example NEON_FOX#0420) and we will delete it for you.
