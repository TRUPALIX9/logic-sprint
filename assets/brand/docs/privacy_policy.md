# Privacy Policy

LogicSprint works offline. Scores and settings are stored only on your device.

If you choose to submit a score to the global leaderboard, the app stores your display name, score, game type, difficulty, app version, and submission time in Firebase Cloud Firestore. No login is required.

The app does not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.

LogicSprint does not use login, backend servers (other than Firestore for optional leaderboard), analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking in version 1.
