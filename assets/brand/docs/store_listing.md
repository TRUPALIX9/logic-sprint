## Full description
LogicSprint is a free brain game app with four quick games that test your reflexes, memory, arithmetic and focus.

Pick a game, play a short round, and beat your best score — no account needed.

Games:
- **Rocket Launch**: Steer your rocket through an asteroid field with a touch of your finger.
- **Memory Lane**: Watch tiles light up on a 3x3, 4x4 or 5x5 grid and tap them back in the same order.
- **Quick Math**: Solve addition, subtraction, multiplication and division problems as fast as you can.
- **Guess Color**: A color word appears in a different ink — tap the ink color, not the word.

Features:
- Easy, Medium and Hard for every game
- Personal bests saved on your device
- Optional global Top 10 leaderboard
- Plays offline
- No login required

Contains ads.
