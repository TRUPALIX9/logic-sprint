## Full description
LogicSprint is a free offline brain game app with quick mini-games designed to test your speed, spatial focus, coordination, and memory.

Play short 30-second challenges, beat your high score, and train your brain anytime.

Features:
- **Rocket Launch**: Dodge incoming asteroids to navigate your rocket ship through space. Supports both touch dragging and custom physical steering arrow buttons.
- **Memory Lane**: Watch sequentially flashing block patterns on a grid (3x3, 4x4, or 5x5) and repeat the exact order with spring animations and rich tactile feedback.
- 30-second timed rounds
- Optional global Top 100 leaderboard (fully cached and optimized)
- Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels
- Offline play support
- Local high scores per game and difficulty
- Custom physical 3D button animations
- Dynamic ad options (Simulated, Real, or completely Disabled)
- No login required

LogicSprint is simple, fast, and fun for quick daily brain practice.
