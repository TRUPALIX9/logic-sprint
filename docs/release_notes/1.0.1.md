## What's new in 1.0.1

**Rocket Launch**
- Pick your ship: Rocket, UFO, Spaceship or Missile, each with its own animation
- Five colour themes, one every 1250 points (ship, then asteroids, then space fade in), with high-contrast asteroids
- Distant planets drift by (gas giant, ringed planet, moon world, cloud world), shooting stars streak across and can burst on a planet
- A gentler speed-up: a sharp player can reach about 10,000
- A little easter egg at each colour change

**Memory Lane**
- The pattern grows by one tile per level instead of starting over
- Red corners while the pattern plays, teal when it's your turn
- Playback speeds up and the level bonus grows along a curve

**All games**
- A one-row top bar: pause / resume, a banner and the score
- The phone's back button pauses the game instead of quitting

**Leaderboard**
- Names carry a 4-digit code (NAME#1234), so names can repeat; "Find a free code" picks one
- Boards update for everyone at 00:00 UTC; your own new best shows right away
- "Refresh ▶": watch a short video to pull everyone's latest scores now (30 min cooldown)
- Your row shines when it appears
