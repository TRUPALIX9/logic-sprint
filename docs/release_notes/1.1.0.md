## What's new in 1.1.0

**New games**
- Angle Sense (Spatial): read an angle and lock it in, or drag the arm to match. Clocks, pies and a compass round on Medium. Within 3° counts as Sharp.
- Line Split (Estimation): touch where a fraction or decimal falls on the number line. You see exactly how close you were.
- Both games give you 2 shields per run; a revive gives one back.

**Profile**
- Sprint Score: one "Top X%" across all six games, shown once enough players are rated.
- Every game's best with its board rank, a run History, and one Share button.

**Also new**
- A new loading screen that launches into the games.
- A 3-2-1 countdown after every revive.
- Ranks: switch games from one board, with your own row pinned.
- Guess Color: clearer buttons and a clearer COLOR | TEXT switch.

**Before release**
- Run `supabase/schema.sql` (new games and Sprint Score) on the production project.
